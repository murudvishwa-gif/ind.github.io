Material Selection for Demanding Industrial Applications

Balance strength, machinability, and operating conditions from the beginning.

All articles

Stackly Editorial · 3 min read

Understand the application

Material selection starts with the environment in which the component will work. Consider loading, temperature, corrosion exposure, and how the part interacts with the surrounding assembly.

Consider manufacturing early

Machinability, stock availability, and finishing requirements influence the production plan. Discuss these constraints with your manufacturing team before the drawing is finalized.

Document the decision

Record the required material grade and any inspection documentation in the project specification so everyone works to the same requirements.

Let’s discuss your application

Every project has its own requirements. Share yours with our team to explore a suitable manufacturing approach.
