Frequently asked questions

Common Client Inquiries

What information do I need for a quotation?

Please share your drawing or specification, material, quantities, preferred finish, and target delivery date. Our team can help clarify any open requirements.

Can you help with a prototype project?

Contact us with your concept or drawing. We can discuss the appropriate manufacturing approach and what is needed to develop your prototype.

Do you offer engineering support?

We can discuss material choices, manufacturability, and process requirements alongside your design team.

How is a project timeline confirmed?

A timeline depends on the scope, materials, quantities, and inspection requirements. We confirm these details during the quotation process.

Can I share project files with the team?

Yes. Prepare an enquiry using the form above and attach drawings or other relevant files in your email app before sending.
