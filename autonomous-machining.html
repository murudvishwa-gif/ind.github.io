A connected production floor

Automation brings repeatable machine tasks, inspection feedback, and production information closer together. The useful starting point is a well-understood process with clearly defined inputs and acceptance criteria.

Start with the process

Before adding automation, review part loading, tooling, workholding, and inspection. Stable processes make it easier to identify where automation can reduce repetitive work and improve consistency.

Keep people in the loop

Operators and engineers remain central to troubleshooting, setup, and process improvement. Clear alerts and accessible production data help teams make informed decisions.

Let’s discuss your application

Every project has its own requirements. Share yours with our team to explore a suitable manufacturing approach.

Before an automation pilot

Use these prompts to prepare a focused discussion with your engineering and production teams.

  • Choose a repeatable operation with a clear acceptance standard.
  • Record setup, loading and inspection steps before changing the process.
  • Agree who reviews alerts and when an operator should intervene.
