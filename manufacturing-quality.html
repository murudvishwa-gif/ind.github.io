Building Quality Into Every Production Stage

Why inspection works best as part of the process, from drawing to dispatch.

All articles

Stackly Editorial · 3 min read

Agree on acceptance criteria

Quality planning begins with a shared understanding of the drawing, critical dimensions, and finish requirements. Clarifying these points early reduces uncertainty later.

Inspect at useful checkpoints

Inspection during production helps identify variations while there is still time to act. Choose checkpoints based on the process and the features that matter most.

Close the feedback loop

Document findings and use them to improve future setups. A consistent record makes recurring issues easier to recognize and address.

Let’s discuss your application

Every project has its own requirements. Share yours with our team to explore a suitable manufacturing approach.

Talk to our team

Prepare an inspection plan

Use these prompts to prepare a focused discussion with your engineering and production teams.

  • Identify critical dimensions and their acceptance criteria.
  • Agree the measurement method and the stage at which checks occur.
  • Keep results linked to the part revision and production batch.
