Getting Your Design Ready for CNC Machining

Practical considerations for a smoother transition from CAD to production.

All articles

Stackly Editorial · 3 min read

Design for tool access

Review whether tools can reach each feature and whether the part can be held securely. Deep cavities and difficult access may require a different machining strategy.

Use purposeful tolerances

Specify tight tolerances where function requires them. Unnecessary constraints can complicate manufacturing and inspection without helping the product.

Share the full picture

Provide drawings alongside your model, including materials, finishes, and critical requirements. A clear handoff helps the manufacturer plan effectively.

Let’s discuss your application

Every project has its own requirements. Share yours with our team to explore a suitable manufacturing approach.

Talk to our team

Before releasing your drawing

Use these prompts to prepare a focused discussion with your engineering and production teams.

  • Mark the critical datums and avoid unnecessary tight tolerances.
  • Check tool access, internal corners and workholding surfaces.
  • Keep the model, drawing revision and material specification aligned.
